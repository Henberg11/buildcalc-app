## Full description (4000 char max)

Stop guessing how much material to buy. BuildCalc puts 10 essential construction and home-improvement calculators in one simple, offline app — built for contractors, DIYers, and weekend renovators who want a fast, accurate answer without digging through spreadsheets or searching the web every time.

WHAT'S INSIDE
🧱 Concrete Calculator — cubic yards and bags needed for slabs, footings & posts
🎨 Paint Calculator — gallons needed based on wall area and coats
🧱 Tile Calculator — tiles needed with a built-in waste allowance
🚪 Drywall Calculator — sheets needed for walls and ceilings
🌿 Mulch & Soil Calculator — cubic yards for beds and landscaping
📜 Wallpaper Calculator — rolls needed per room
🪵 Flooring Calculator — boxes needed for laminate, hardwood or vinyl
🚧 Fencing Calculator — posts and panels for any perimeter
🪵 Board Feet Calculator — lumber volume and project cost for woodworking
🏠 Roofing Calculator — squares and bundles of shingles needed

WHY BUILDCALC
• Fast — enter your measurements, get an answer in one tap
• Works offline — no signal needed on the job site
• No account, no clutter, no sign-up
• Built-in waste allowances so you don't come up short
• One app instead of ten different websites

Whether you're pouring a patio, painting a bedroom, laying tile, or pricing out a lumber run, BuildCalc gives you a straight answer fast so you can get back to the job.
