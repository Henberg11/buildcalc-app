Privacy Policy for BuildCalc

Last updated: July 24, 2026

BuildCalc ("the app") is developed and maintained by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

BuildCalc does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers, and does not store the numbers you enter into any calculator — all calculations happen locally on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Advertising

BuildCalc displays ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, and app usage data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization on Android).

Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party service, which may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob

Children's Privacy

BuildCalc is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy
